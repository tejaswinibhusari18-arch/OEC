7. Business Improvement Suggestions

  1. Improve Google Business Profile: Keep photos, timings, new arrivals and contact details updated.
  2. Collect Customer Reviews: Ask happy customers for genuine Google reviews after purchase.
  3. Create Short Videos: Share unboxing, camera tests and quick comparison Reels.
  4. Referral Benefits: Give a small accessory discount when a customer refers a friend.
  5. Easy Enquiry Options: Offer WhatsApp catalogue and a simple enquiry form.
  6. Flexible Payment Plans: Promote EMI, exchange and festive cashback offers.
  7. Launch Events: Organise in-store demo days for new phone launches.
  8. Measure Results: Track reach, enquiries, store visits and sales to see which marketing works best.
